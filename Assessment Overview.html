Cumulative Assessment Overview

ENG 6 Spring 2026

When and Where

Stage 1 takes place during Week 8 (Tuesday–Thursday, before Memorial Weekend) as a 50-minute proctored session in MATLAB Grader. Arrive at least 10 minutes before your scheduled start time; check-in begins 10 minutes before the assessment opens. Leave all personal items, including cellphones, at the front of the room. No additional materials are permitted.

What is Covered

Lectures 1 through 10. Lecture 9 (GUI) is excluded: you will not be asked to create a graphical user interface. For the detailed topic list per lecture, see Lecture Essentials.

The assessment is closed book with no notes. The full MATLAB documentation is available inside the lockdown browser. The MATLAB AI assistant is not.

Format (100 points, 50 minutes)

Format (100 points, 50 minutes)
Section Content Points
A 5 multiple-choice questions: 3 OOP + 2 conceptual probes 25
B 3 short MATLAB Grader coding problems 50
C 1 integrative MATLAB Grader coding problem 25

Sections A and B together reach the 75-point pass threshold. Section C is the differentiator for higher grades.

Two submission attempts per problem in Sections B and C. First-attempt feedback is visible. The last submission is the one that is graded. Develop and test your code in MATLAB before submitting to MATLAB Grader.

Final grades are determined on Friday of Week 8.

Grading and Retake Policy

This assessment has two stages. Stage 1 is required; Stage 2 is optional.

Stage 2 — Finals Week (Optional Retake). A different, cumulative exam covering the original 6 lectures plus Weeks 8–9 material in equal proportion. One submission attempt, no feedback. Maximum score: 80%. Sign-up required one week in advance.

Final grade = the higher of your two scores (80% cap applied to Stage 2 only). Taking the retake cannot lower your grade.

Grading and Retake Policy
Scenario Stage 1 Stage 2 Final Grade
Did well on Stage 1 95% — 95%
Strong comeback 45% 90% → capped 80%
Slight boost 72% 85% → capped 80%
Off day on retake 75% 60% 75%

There is no compulsory final.

How to Prepare

Three documents, in this order:

  1. Fundamental Concepts Mindmap — start here. Seven concepts that run through every lecture and every Mini-Project. Work through the self-check questions actively.
  2. Lecture Essentials — use alongside the Mindmap to identify specific gaps per lecture.
  3. Practice Study Guide — worked MATLAB examples and exam-day mechanics. Use once you have worked through the Mindmap.
