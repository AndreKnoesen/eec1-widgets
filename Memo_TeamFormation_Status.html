Check the posted team list

The confirmed team list is posted here: https://aknoesen.github.io/eec1-widgets/team_report_public.html

Look for your name. There are two possible situations:

Your name is on the list

Good — your team declaration was received and confirmed. Please verify that your team members and project track are recorded correctly.

  • If everything looks correct: no further action is needed.
  • If anything is wrong (wrong teammate, wrong track, or wrong role): all three team members must resubmit the team declaration survey. In the final question ("Any questions or concerns?") write "CORRECTION" and briefly describe what needs to change. The last submission received before the deadline is what counts.

Your name is not on the list

Your team was not successfully confirmed. This happens for one of two reasons:

  1. Your team was not fully mutual. A team is only confirmed when all three members have each other listed in their submissions. If even one member listed different teammates, or left a field blank, the team cannot be confirmed.

  2. You have not yet submitted the team declaration survey.

In either case, you must act now. Form or reconfirm your team and submit the survey before 5:00 PM today:

Survey link: https://forms.gle/FgbUYtN1uFU9TQGA7
(Sign in with your UC Davis account.)

Reminder: Teams must be exactly three members. Four-member teams are permitted only in exceptional circumstances and must be approved. Single- and two-person teams are not allowed under any circumstances.

Deadline

5:00 PM today, Wednesday May 7. Students who do not have a confirmed team by the deadline will be assigned to a team by the instructional staff. Students who are assigned rather than self-selected may have less flexibility in track and role preference.
