L11 Lab — Group Project Data Collection

ENG 6: Engineering Problem Solving with Applied AI | Spring 2026

What You Will Do

You will record 20 seconds of smartphone accelerometer data for three physical activities — walking, running, and jumping — using a MATLAB script that guides you through every step. The script saves three files. You upload them to a shared class folder. In Lecture 12, your batch analysis loop will process the entire class dataset.

Total time: ~5 minutes of active recording. The rest of the 30-minute lab is setup, upload, and helping teammates.

Before You Run the Script

On your phone:
1. Install MATLAB Mobile from the App Store or Google Play (free — use your UC Davis MathWorks account)
2. Open the app and sign in with the same account you use for MATLAB Online
3. Connect to Wi-Fi (same network as your laptop — eduroam works)
4. In the app: tap Settings → Connect and pair with your MATLAB Online session

In MATLAB Online:
1. Download the script by pasting this into the Command Window and pressing Enter:
matlab websave("L11_Lab_DataCollection.m", "https://aknoesen.github.io/eec1-widgets/L11_Lab_DataCollection.m");
The script saves to your current folder. You can also download it directly at:
https://aknoesen.github.io/eec1-widgets/L11_Lab_DataCollection.m
2. Make sure your current folder in MATLAB is set to a location you can find (e.g., your MATLAB Drive home folder) — this is where your files will be saved

Running the Script

Type this in the MATLAB Command Window and press Enter:

L11_Lab_DataCollection

The script will guide you through every step. Here is what to expect:

Step 1 — Setup prompt
The script asks you to confirm your phone is connected. Check that MATLAB Mobile shows "Connected" before pressing Enter.

Step 2 — Enter your information
You will be asked for:
- First name
- Last name
- Lab section (e.g., A01 — use the code shown on the projector)

Enter these exactly. Your files will be named:

walk_firstname_lastname_section.mat
run_firstname_lastname_section.mat
jump_firstname_lastname_section.mat

Step 3 — Three recordings
For each activity, the script prints instructions, then waits for you to press Enter. Once you press Enter:
- Hold the phone in your hand (portrait orientation, screen facing you)
- A 20-second countdown appears: 20 19 18 ...
- Perform the activity continuously for the full countdown
- The script stops automatically and saves the file

Running the Script
Activity What to do
Walking Walk at a normal pace, continuously and in a straight line or loop
Running Run at a moderate pace — clearly different from walking
Jumping Jump in place repeatedly, approximately once per second

Tips for clean data:
- Keep the phone in your hand — do not put it in your pocket
- Start moving before the countdown begins, not after
- Stay consistent throughout the 20 seconds

Step 4 — Confirmation
After each recording the script prints the filename, sample count, and recording duration. A typical result:

Saved: walk_jane_smith_a03.mat  |  2014 samples  |  20.1 s  |  100 Hz

If you see WARNING: No data received — check your phone connection and re-run the script.

Uploading Your Files

After all three recordings:

  1. In MATLAB Online, go to the Files panel and find your three .mat files
  2. Upload them to the shared class folder — your TA will post the path in the course chat
  3. Confirm your files appear in the shared folder before you leave

What Happens Next

  • Lecture 12 (Thursday): you will design a results structure and extend the batch loop using synthetic data — the same format as the files you collected today. Bring your uploaded files so you can reference your own data during the activity.
  • All labs conclude Monday: once every section has uploaded, your TA will post the complete files array listing everyone's recordings.
  • In the homework: paste the files array into your batch analysis script — the loop from Lecture 11 processes the full class dataset (your files and your classmates') without any other changes to the loop body.
