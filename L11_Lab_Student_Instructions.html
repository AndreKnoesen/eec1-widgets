Lab 11: Group Project Data Collection

This lab produces the raw material for everything that follows. You will record accelerometer data for three activities — walking, running, and jumping — submit three files to Canvas, and be done. In Lab 12 you will design the data structure that holds the results. In the lab the following week you will run your batch analysis loop on the full class dataset: 492 recordings from 164 students, including the three you collect today.

Goals

By the end of this lab session you will have:

  • Recorded 20 seconds of smartphone accelerometer data for walking, running, and jumping
  • Saved three correctly named .mat files to your MATLAB Drive
  • Submitted all three files to Canvas before leaving

Total active recording time: ~5 minutes. The rest of the 30-minute session is setup, submission, and helping teammates.

This lab is completed entirely in your lab session. There is no pre-lab or post-lab component.

Background: What the Script Does and Why the Filename Matters

Read before starting Step 1

The data collection script guides you through three 20-second recordings. After each one it saves a .mat file that contains a single variable data — an N×4 matrix with columns [Ax, Ay, Az, t]: three axes of acceleration in m/s² and a time vector in seconds sampled at 100 Hz.

The script names each file from the information you enter:

walk_firstname_lastname_section.mat
run_firstname_lastname_section.mat
jump_firstname_lastname_section.mat

The naming convention matters. In Lab 12, your batch analysis loop will extract the activity type and your name directly from these filenames using string operations. A typo in your name or section code will cause your files to be attributed incorrectly or skipped. Enter exactly what is shown on the projector.

Step 1 — Connect Your Phone to MATLAB

  1. Open MATLAB Mobile on your phone and sign in with the same MathWorks account you use for MATLAB Online
  2. Connect to Wi-Fi — use the same network as your laptop (eduroam works)
  3. In the app: tap Settings → Connect and pair with your MATLAB Online session
  4. Confirm the app shows “Connected” before proceeding

If your phone won’t connect after 2 minutes: pair with a teammate who has a working connection. One phone per team is enough — you will each enter your own name and section when the script prompts, and save your own files.

Step 2 — Download the Data Collection Script

In MATLAB Online, paste this into the Command Window and press Enter:

websave("L11_Lab_DataCollection.m", "https://aknoesen.github.io/eec1-widgets/L11_Lab_DataCollection.m");

Confirm the file appears in your current MATLAB Drive folder. Set your current folder to a location you can find easily (e.g., your MATLAB Drive home folder) — this is where your three .mat files will be saved.

Fallback: if websave fails, open the URL directly in your browser and download the file manually: https://aknoesen.github.io/eec1-widgets/L11_Lab_DataCollection.m

Step 3 — Run the Script and Record Your Data

Type this in the Command Window and press Enter:

L11_Lab_DataCollection

The script guides you through every step. Here is what to expect:

Setup prompt. The script asks you to confirm your phone is connected. Press Enter only after MATLAB Mobile shows “Connected.”

Enter your information. You will be prompted for your first name, last name, and lab section code (use the code shown on the projector, e.g., A01). The script lowercases everything automatically. Your three filenames will be shown before recording begins — confirm they look correct.

Three recordings. For each activity the script prints instructions, waits for you to press Enter, then counts down 20 seconds:

Activity What to do
Walking Walk at a normal pace, continuously, in a straight line or loop
Running Run at a moderate pace — clearly different from walking
Jumping Jump in place repeatedly, approximately once per second

Tips for clean data: - Hold the phone in your hand (portrait orientation, screen facing you) — do not put it in a pocket - Start moving before the countdown begins, not after - Stay consistent throughout the full 20 seconds

Confirmation after each recording. The script prints the filename, sample count, and duration:

Saved: walk_jane_smith_a03.mat  |  2014 samples  |  20.1 s  |  100 Hz

If you see WARNING: No data received — check your phone connection and re-run the script from the beginning: clc; L11_Lab_DataCollection

Final summary. After all three recordings the script prints:

=== Ready to submit ===
Attach these three files to the Canvas assignment:
  walk_jane_smith_a03.mat
  run_jane_smith_a03.mat
  jump_jane_smith_a03.mat

Step 4 — Submit Your Files to Canvas

  1. Go to Canvas → Assignments → L11 Lab Sensor Data
  2. Click Submit Assignment
  3. Click Choose Files — select all three .mat files listed in the script summary above
  4. Click Submit
  5. Confirm Canvas shows “Submitted” with all three filenames listed

Before you leave: if you have any trouble submitting in Canvas, email all three files directly to your TA before midnight. Do not leave without confirming your submission or notifying your TA.
