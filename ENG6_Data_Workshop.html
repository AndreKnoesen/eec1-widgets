Switching Between Methods

- Record both Acceleration and Positioning with MATLAB’s mobile app via Sensors and switch between the two - Mapping Toolbox may be useful for GPS Operations - Switching should depend on set threshold for GPS/Positioning Horizontal Accuracy measurement, which suffers indoors - In my experience, ~20m is appropriate. Accuracy can be as good as 6m, approaching around 15m once closer to Kemper

Several Options to Improve Your Solution!

- You’re encouraged to explore, though of course keep timelines in mind! - I’ll now go over a function you can use to SUPPLEMENT (not substitute!) bias subtraction for cleaner readings: accelcal! - Requires either the Sensor Fusion and Tracking Toolbox or the Navigation Toolbox
![](media/s06_a.png)

accelcal

After using `accellog` to record data at standstill at each of the orientations, you can call: 
[A, b] = accelcal(XUP, XDOWN, YUP, YDOWN, ZUP, ZDOWN)
Then, to apply calibration to `accellog` data, you can use: 
(data)*A + b
Also apply to the resting data before taking mean and performing bias subtraction. ![](media/s07_a.png)
